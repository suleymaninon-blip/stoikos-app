CAMERA: behind him and slightly to the side, at shoulder height, following him. We see his back and his cloak. He never turns to face the camera.

0–3 s: from behind, he walks calmly and steadily along the stone path through the storm while the wind whips his cloak and spray bursts over the rocks beside him. He does not hurry and does not shield his face.

6–8 s: he stops in front of the closed door and places one hand flat on the wet wood. End on this moment, with "STOIKOS" clearly visible above him. The door stays closed.

STYLE: photorealistic, cinematic, shot on 35mm film, natural film grain. Cold dark blue-grey storm palette; the only warm light comes from the lighthouse window. Handheld camera with slight shake from the wind. Single continuous shot, no cuts, no transitions. Vertical 9:16.

DO NOT SHOW: a studio portrait or grey background; any boats, people, buildings other than the lighthouse; any text, sign or logo other than "STOIKOS"; the door opening; sunrise or calm sea.
